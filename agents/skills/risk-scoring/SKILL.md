---
name: risk-scoring
description: How the team writes and scores a risk (cause -> event -> effect, probability x impact).
---
Write every risk as: "Because <cause>, <event> may happen, which would lead to <effect>."
Probability: 1 Low (unlikely this sprint) · 2 Medium (could happen this sprint) · 3 High (already happening or very likely).
Impact: 1 Low (minor delay) · 2 Medium (a task slips, milestone still reachable) · 3 High (a course milestone, a safety rule, a legal/ethical point or the budget total is threatened).
Score = probability x impact (1-9). Score >= 6: escalate to the owner the same day. Score 9: whole team before the weekly review.
