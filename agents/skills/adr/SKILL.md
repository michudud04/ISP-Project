---
name: adr
description: How to draft an Architectural Decision Record when a technical choice changes.
---
Draft an ADR only when meeting notes show a technical choice was made or changed (a line starting with "Decision:" or "We decided").
Format: Title · Status (Proposed — a human sets Accepted) · Context · Decision · Alternatives considered · Consequences (including at least one trade-off) · Source (meeting file).
Never mark an ADR as Accepted yourself.
